Who we are

A label built around the needle, not the printer.

Alif Noor Dresses is the girls' wear line from Ayat Creation. The name comes from two words that anchor how we design: Alif, the first letter — a beginning — and Noor, light. Every piece is meant to be a child's first taste of dressing up for something that matters.

We work in small runs, focused on hand embroidery — chikankari, threadwork and zardozi — rather than printed patterns, so the detail holds up under a close look, not just in a photograph.

Every order is handled personally over WhatsApp, directly with the atelier, so sizing and styling questions get a real answer, not an automated one.

How a piece is made

From sketch to send-off.

01

Design & colour study

Every style starts with a silhouette and a palette chosen to suit the occasion it's meant for — daily wear, festive or bridal function.

02

Hand embroidery

Threadwork, chikankari or zardozi is worked onto the fabric panel by panel before the garment is cut and stitched.

03

Fit & finish

Seams, hems and linings are checked for comfort against how children actually move — running, sitting, playing — not just standing still.

04

Order on WhatsApp

You message us the style, size and city. We confirm price, stitching time and delivery — one conversation, no separate account.

What we hold to

Three things we don't compromise on.

A

Hand over machine

Embroidery is worked by hand wherever the design calls for it — it takes longer, but it's the difference between a printed motif and a real one.

N

Made for children

Necklines, sleeve lengths and hem weight are all considered for how a child wears clothes — not scaled-down adult patterns.

D

Direct relationships

No marketplace, no reseller. Every order goes straight to Ayat Creation, so questions get answered by the people who made the piece.
